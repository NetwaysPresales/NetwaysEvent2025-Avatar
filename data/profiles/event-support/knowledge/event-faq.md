# Netways 2025 Summit FAQs

## Where is the event taking place?

The Netways 2025 Tech Summit is held at the Grand Convention Center, Hall A and B. The address is 123 Innovation Drive, Tech City.

## What time does registration open?

Registration opens daily at 8:00 AM in the main lobby. Please bring your QR code and ID.

## Is there Wi-Fi available?

Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is free for all attendees.
**Network Name:** NetwaysSummit2025
**Password:** FutureIsNow

## Where can I find the lunch area?

Lunch is served in the Sapphire Ballroom on the 2nd floor from 12:00 PM to 1:30 PM. Vegetarian and vegan options are available.

## How do I download the event app?

You can search for "Netways Events" on the App Store or Google Play Store. The app includes the full agenda, speaker bios, and live polling.
