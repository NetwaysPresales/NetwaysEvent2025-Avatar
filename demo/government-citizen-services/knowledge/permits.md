# Permits & Licenses Guide

## Business Permits

### Trade License
- **Required For**: All commercial business activities
- **Validity**: 1 year (renewable)
- **Fee**: $500-$5,000 (based on business type and size)
- **Processing Time**: 5-10 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Business registration certificate
  - Commercial lease agreement or property ownership
  - Identity documents of owner/partners
  - Business activity description
  - Health and safety compliance (if applicable)

### Food Service License
- **Required For**: Restaurants, cafes, food vendors
- **Validity**: 1 year
- **Fee**: $1,000
- **Processing Time**: 10-15 business days
- **Additional Requirements**:
  - Health department inspection
  - Food handler certifications
  - Kitchen facility compliance certificate
  - Fire safety clearance

### Construction License
- **Required For**: Construction companies, contractors
- **Validity**: 2 years
- **Fee**: $2,000-$10,000 (based on company size)
- **Processing Time**: 15-20 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Engineering qualifications
  - Insurance certificates
  - Safety compliance records
  - Previous project references

## Personal Permits

### Residence Permit
- **Required For**: Non-citizens staying longer than 90 days
- **Validity**: 1-3 years (based on visa type)
- **Fee**: $200-$500
- **Processing Time**: 10-15 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Valid passport and entry visa
  - Employment contract or sponsor letter
  - Medical clearance certificate
  - Proof of accommodation
  - Financial solvency proof

### Work Permit
- **Required For**: Foreign nationals working in the country
- **Validity**: 1-2 years (tied to employment contract)
- **Fee**: $300
- **Processing Time**: 15-20 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Employment offer letter
  - Educational qualifications (attested)
  - Medical clearance
  - Background check clearance
  - Employer sponsorship

### Marriage License
- **Required For**: Legal marriage ceremonies
- **Validity**: 90 days from issue date
- **Fee**: $50
- **Processing Time**: 3-5 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Both parties' identity documents
  - Birth certificates
  - Single status certificate (if previously unmarried)
  - Divorce decree (if applicable)
  - Witnesses (2 required)

## Event Permits

### Public Event Permit
- **Required For**: Public gatherings, festivals, concerts
- **Fee**: $500-$2,000 (based on event size)
- **Processing Time**: 10-15 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Event details and schedule
  - Venue agreement
  - Security plan
  - Traffic management plan (if applicable)
  - Insurance certificate
  - Noise permit (if applicable)

### Street Closure Permit
- **Required For**: Block parties, parades, construction
- **Fee**: $200-$1,000
- **Processing Time**: 7-10 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Detailed route/area map
  - Alternative traffic routes
  - Notification plan for affected residents
  - Safety measures plan

## Property Permits

### Home Renovation Permit
- **Required For**: Major renovations, additions, structural changes
- **Fee**: Based on project value (0.5-1%)
- **Processing Time**: 10-15 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Property ownership documents
  - Detailed renovation plans
  - Structural engineering report (if structural changes)
  - Contractor license
  - Insurance certificate

### Signage Permit
- **Required For**: Commercial signs, billboards
- **Fee**: $100-$500 (based on size and location)
- **Processing Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Requirements**:
  - Sign design and dimensions
  - Location plan
  - Property owner consent
  - Safety compliance certificate

## Application Process

### General Steps
1. **Prepare Documents**: Gather all required documents
2. **Complete Application**: Fill out official application form (online or paper)
3. **Submit Application**: Submit with all documents and fees
4. **Review Process**: Department reviews application (processing time varies)
5. **Inspection** (if required): On-site inspection scheduled
6. **Approval**: Receive permit/license or request for additional information
7. **Collection**: Pick up permit/license or receive digitally

### Online Application
- Available for most permits/licenses
- Upload documents digitally
- Track application status
- Receive notifications via email/SMS
- Digital permit/license delivery

### In-Person Application
- Visit appropriate government office
- Bring original documents and copies
- Pay fees (cash, card, or bank transfer accepted)
- Receive receipt with tracking number

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Missing Documents
- **Issue**: Application incomplete
- **Solution**: Department will notify you within 5 business days to provide missing documents
- **Extension**: 30 days to provide missing documents before application cancellation

### Rejection
- **Issue**: Application doesn't meet requirements
- **Solution**: Review rejection reasons, address issues, resubmit
- **Appeal**: Available within 30 days of rejection

### Expired Permits
- **Issue**: Permit expired before use
- **Solution**: Reapply (fees may apply)
- **Prevention**: Use permit within validity period

## Fees & Payment

### Payment Methods
- Online: Credit/debit card, bank transfer
- In-person: Cash, card, bank transfer
- Government payment centers: Available throughout the city

### Fee Waivers
- Available for low-income individuals (with proof)
- Non-profit organizations (with certification)
- Government entities

### Refunds
- Processing fees: Non-refundable
- Permit fees: Refundable if application withdrawn before processing
- Refund processing: 10-15 business days
