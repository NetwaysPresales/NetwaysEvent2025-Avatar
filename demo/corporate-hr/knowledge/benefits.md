# Employee Benefits Overview

## Health Insurance

### Medical Coverage
- **Plan Options**: Three tiers available (Basic, Standard, Premium)
- **Coverage**: Medical, dental, vision, and mental health services
- **Deductibles**: Range from $500 (Premium) to $2,000 (Basic)
- **Open Enrollment**: November 1-15 annually
- **New Hire Enrollment**: Within 30 days of start date

### Dental & Vision
- Included in all medical plans
- Annual cleanings covered at 100%
- Orthodontic coverage available (lifetime maximum: $2,500)

### Mental Health & Wellness
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): 24/7 confidential counseling
- Mental health coverage: Same as medical coverage
- Wellness programs: Gym reimbursement up to $50/month, nutrition counseling

## Retirement Plans

### 401(k) Plan
- **Company Match**: 100% match up to 6% of salary
- **Vesting**: Immediate vesting for company contributions
- **Contribution Limits**: Up to IRS maximum ($23,000 for 2024)
- **Investment Options**: 15+ diversified funds available

### Pension Plan (Legacy)
- Available for employees hired before 2015
- Contact HR for eligibility and details

## Time Off & Leave

### Paid Time Off (PTO)
- **Accrual Rate**: Based on years of service
  - 0-2 years: 15 days/year
  - 3-5 years: 20 days/year
  - 6+ years: 25 days/year
- **Carryover**: Up to 5 days can be carried to next year
- **Request Process**: Submit through HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance

### Sick Leave
- **Accrual**: 10 days/year
- **Unlimited**: For extended medical conditions (with documentation)

### Parental Leave
- **Maternity/Paternity**: 12 weeks paid leave
- **Adoption**: 12 weeks paid leave
- **Additional**: Can use PTO for extended leave

### Bereavement Leave
- **Immediate Family**: 5 days paid
- **Extended Family**: 3 days paid

## Professional Development

### Tuition Reimbursement
- Up to $5,250/year for job-related courses
- Must maintain B average or better
- Reimbursement after course completion

### Training & Certifications
- Company-sponsored training programs
- Certification exam fees covered
- Conference attendance support (case-by-case)

### Internal Mobility
- Job posting portal for internal opportunities
- Mentorship programs available
- Leadership development programs

## Additional Benefits

### Commuter Benefits
- Pre-tax transit passes
- Parking reimbursement (select locations)
- Bike-to-work program: $20/month reimbursement

### Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)
- Healthcare FSA: Up to $3,200/year
- Dependent Care FSA: Up to $5,000/year
- Use-it-or-lose-it: Funds expire at year-end

### Life Insurance
- Basic coverage: 1x annual salary (company-paid)
- Optional coverage: Up to 5x annual salary (employee-paid)
- Spouse/child coverage available

### Disability Insurance
- Short-term disability: 60% salary for up to 26 weeks
- Long-term disability: 60% salary after 26 weeks
- Both company-paid
