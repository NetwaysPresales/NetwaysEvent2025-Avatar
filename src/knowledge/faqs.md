# Netways FAQs

## General

**Q: What is Netways?**
A: Netways is a leading technology consultancy and solutions provider specializing in digital transformation, cloud and managed services, and enterprise content management. With over 20 years of experience, we serve governments and organizations primarily in the MENA region and North America. Use us to modernize your business processes and leverage intelligent cloud platforms.

**Q: What are your main areas of expertise?**
A: We focus on three core pillars:
1.  **Digital Transformation**: We help you adopt "Systems of Intelligence" to differentiate your business and modernize applications.
2.  **Cloud & Managed Services**: As a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider (CSP), we manage your IT operations on Azure, Office 365, and hybrid environments.
3.  **Enterprise Content Management (ECM)**: We integrate and manage your enterprise content to ensure information flows efficiently across your organization.
