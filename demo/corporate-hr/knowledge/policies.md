# Company Policies & Procedures

## Code of Conduct

### Professional Behavior
- Treat all colleagues with respect and dignity
- Maintain confidentiality of company information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report unethical behavior through proper channels

### Anti-Discrimination & Harassment
- Zero tolerance for discrimination or harassment
- Protected categories: Race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation
- Reporting: Contact HR or use anonymous hotline
- Retaliation is prohibited

## Remote Work Policy

### Eligibility
- Based on role and manager approval
- Must have suitable home workspace
- Reliable internet connection required

### Expectations
- Maintain same productivity standards as office
- Available during core business hours (9 AM - 5 PM local time)
- Attend required in-person meetings
- Follow all security protocols

### Hybrid Schedule
- Standard: 3 days in office, 2 days remote
- Flexible arrangements available with manager approval
- Office space booking system for in-office days

## Attendance & Punctuality

### Standard Hours
- Full-time: 40 hours/week
- Core hours: 10 AM - 3 PM (must be present)
- Flexible start/end times: 7 AM - 10 AM start, 4 PM - 7 PM end

### Tardiness
- Notify manager if running late
- Excessive tardiness may result in disciplinary action
- Use time tracking system accurately

## Dress Code

### Office Attire
- Business casual standard
- Professional appearance expected
- No offensive or inappropriate clothing
- Client-facing roles: Business professional

### Remote Work
- Professional appearance for video calls
- Appropriate background for video meetings

## Technology & Security

### Equipment
- Company-issued laptops and phones
- Personal device use: Limited and subject to security policies
- Equipment return: Required upon termination

### Security Policies
- Strong passwords required (changed every 90 days)
- Two-factor authentication mandatory
- No unauthorized software installation
- Report security incidents immediately

### Data Protection
- Confidential information: Handle with care
- No sharing of company data externally without authorization
- GDPR and data privacy compliance required

## Performance & Reviews

### Annual Reviews
- Conducted in Q4 each year
- Goal setting: Q1
- Mid-year check-ins: Q3
- 360-degree feedback available

### Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
- Used for underperformance
- 30-90 day improvement period
- Regular check-ins with manager and HR

## Disciplinary Actions

### Progressive Discipline
1. Verbal warning
2. Written warning
3. Final written warning
4. Termination

### Immediate Termination
- Theft or fraud
- Violence or threats
- Gross misconduct
- Serious policy violations

## Grievance Procedure

### Informal Resolution
- Discuss with direct manager first
- HR mediation available

### Formal Complaint
- Submit written complaint to HR
- Investigation within 10 business days
- Written response provided
- Appeal process available

## Social Media Policy

### Personal Social Media
- No disclosure of confidential information
- Respectful representation of company
- No defamatory comments about company or colleagues

### Company Social Media
- Authorized spokespersons only
- Marketing approval required for company accounts
