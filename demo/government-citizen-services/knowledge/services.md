# Government Services & Programs

## Business Services

### Business Registration
- **New Business License**: Required for all commercial activities
- **Processing Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Fee**: $500 (standard), $1,000 (premium processing - 2 days)
- **Requirements**: 
  - Business name registration
  - Trade license application
  - Commercial registration certificate
  - Identity documents of owner/partners
- **Renewal**: Annual renewal required (due 30 days before expiration)
- **Online Portal**: Available at business.gov.portal

### Professional Licenses
- **Categories**: Medical, Legal, Engineering, Accounting, and more
- **Application**: Submit through professional licensing portal
- **Requirements**: Vary by profession (education, exams, experience)
- **Processing Time**: 15-30 business days
- **Renewal**: Typically every 2-3 years

## Personal Services

### Identity Documents
- **National ID Card**: 
  - First-time application: 10-15 business days
  - Renewal: 5-7 business days
  - Fee: $25
  - Required: Birth certificate, proof of address, photos

- **Passport**:
  - New application: 15-20 business days
  - Renewal: 10-15 business days
  - Fee: $100 (standard), $150 (express - 5 days)
  - Required: Current ID, photos, application form

### Birth & Death Certificates
- **Birth Certificate**: 
  - Application: Hospital or civil registry office
  - Processing: 3-5 business days
  - Fee: $15
  - Required: Hospital records, parent identification

- **Death Certificate**:
  - Application: Hospital or civil registry office
  - Processing: 2-3 business days
  - Fee: $15
  - Required: Medical certificate, family identification

## Property & Real Estate

### Property Registration
- **Title Deed Registration**: Required for property ownership transfer
- **Processing Time**: 10-15 business days
- **Fee**: 2% of property value
- **Requirements**: 
  - Sale agreement
  - Property survey
  - Tax clearance certificate
  - Identity documents

### Building Permits
- **Residential Construction**: 
  - Application: Building department
  - Processing: 15-20 business days
  - Fee: Based on project value (0.5-2%)
  - Required: Site plans, architectural drawings, engineering reports

- **Commercial Construction**:
  - Processing: 20-30 business days
  - Additional requirements: Environmental impact assessment, traffic study

## Public Benefits

### Social Security
- **Eligibility**: Citizens and legal residents
- **Benefits**: Retirement, disability, survivor benefits
- **Application**: Social Security Administration office or online portal
- **Processing**: 30-60 days for initial applications
- **Required Documents**: ID, birth certificate, employment records

### Healthcare Benefits
- **Public Health Insurance**: 
  - Eligibility: Low-income individuals and families
  - Application: Health department or online portal
  - Coverage: Basic medical, dental, vision
  - Renewal: Annual recertification required

### Education Assistance
- **Student Grants**: 
  - Available for higher education
  - Application: Education department
  - Requirements: Academic records, financial need documentation
  - Deadline: Applications accepted year-round

## Vehicle Services

### Driver's License
- **New License**: 
  - Written test and driving test required
  - Processing: 5-7 business days after passing tests
  - Fee: $50
  - Valid for: 5 years

- **Renewal**: 
  - Can renew up to 6 months before expiration
  - Processing: 3-5 business days
  - Fee: $30
  - Vision test required

### Vehicle Registration
- **New Registration**: 
  - Required within 30 days of purchase
  - Fee: Based on vehicle value
  - Required: Purchase documents, insurance, inspection certificate

- **Annual Renewal**: 
  - Due on registration anniversary
  - Fee: $100 (standard vehicles)
  - Required: Valid insurance, inspection certificate

## Online Services Portal

### Account Registration
- Create account with national ID or email
- Two-factor authentication required
- Access to all online services
- Document storage and history
