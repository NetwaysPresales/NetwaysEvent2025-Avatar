# Investment FAQ

## What is the minimum investment for the Growth Fund?

The minimum initial investment for the Netways Growth Fund is $5,000 USD. Subsequent contributions can be as low as $500.

## How do you manage risk?

We use a diversified approach across asset classes (equities, bonds, real estate) and geographies. Our proprietary AI algorithms monitor market volatility 24/7 to rebalance portfolios automatically.

## What are your fees?

We charge a flat advisory fee of 0.75% annually on assets under management. There are no trading commissions or hidden withdrawal fees.

## Can I withdraw my funds at any time?

Yes, our portfolios are highly liquid. Standard withdrawals typically process within 2-3 business days.

## Do you offer ESG options?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated Sustainable Future Fund that invests exclusively in companies with high Environmental, Social, and Governance ratings.
