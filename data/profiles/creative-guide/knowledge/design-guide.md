# Design Principles & Inspiration

## What is the 60-30-10 Rule?

It's a classic decor rule that helps create a balanced color palette. It suggests using:

* **60%** dominant color (usually a neutral wall color)
* **30%** secondary color (upholstery, curtains)
* **10%** accent color (pillows, art, accessories)

## Principles of Visual Hierarchy

1. **Size**: Larger elements demand more attention.
2. **Color**: Bright or contrasting colors draw the eye.
3. **Space**: Whitespace (negative space) gives elements room to breathe.
4. **Texture**: Tactile or visual texture creates interest.

## What is Glassmorphism?

Glassmorphism is a UI design trend emphasizing light/dark mode compatibility and a frosted-glass effect. It uses:

* Translucency (background blur)
* Vivid colors floating in space
* Subtle, light borders on translucent objects
